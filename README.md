[Best Markets to Advertise in an E-learning Product](Best%20Markets%20to%20Advertise%20in%20an%20E-learning%20Product.ipynb) (guided project) |
Where should an e-learning company spend its ad budget? | The survey has 18,175 responses. 
After cleaning and judging outliers, the US is the clear first market. 
The second market is not settled by the data, and the notebook says so. |

Anti-diabetic drug sales | Which forecasting method holds up on past data? | 
A rolling backtest across four test periods. Triple exponential smoothing won all four, with errors from about 3% to 9%. 
The same method later became [AnalytixTool](https://github.com/paogarciar/analytixtool). |

Amazon stock price analysis | Can US economic indicators help explain a stock price? 
Combined stock prices with US economic data. The best model missed by about 21%, so I describe it as an exploration, not a prediction tool. 

[Credit Card Customer Segmentation](Credit%20Card%20Customer%20Segmentation.ipynb) 
Which groups of customers behave alike? 
K-means with K = 6 on 10,127 customers. Cluster sizes range from 898 to 2,263 customers.
